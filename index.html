📋 Module 1: Understanding CVs

What is a CV?

A CV (Curriculum Vitae) is a document about YOU. It tells employers:

  • ✅ Your name and contact information
  • ✅ Your education (where you studied)
  • ✅ Your work experience (jobs you had)
  • ✅ Your skills (what you can do)
💡 Remember: A good CV helps you get a job interview!

🎓 Module 3: Writing About Education

📖 Learn the Grammar

Present Simple (Now):
✅ I study Engineering at Higher Colleges of Technology.
✅ I am studying Computer Science.

Past Simple (Finished):
✅ I graduated from Al Ain High School in 2022.
✅ I completed my diploma last year.

✍️ Module 5: Writing Professional Sentences

✅ Do's and ❌ Don'ts for CV Language

✅ DO:

  • Use action verbs
  • Be specific
  • Use simple sentences
  • Check spelling
  • Be honest

❌ DON'T:

  • Use slang words
  • Write long sentences
  • Make spelling mistakes
  • Lie about skills
  • Use "I" too much

📝 Practice: Fix the Mistakes

These sentences have problems. Choose the CORRECT version:

Question 1: Which sentence is better for a CV?

A) I'm super good at computers and stuff
B) I have strong computer skills and experience with Microsoft Office

Question 2: Which sentence is better?

A) I worked at a shop and did lots of different things and helped people
B) I worked as a sales assistant. I helped customers and managed inventory.

Question 3: Which sentence uses better action verbs?

A) I did work with social media
B) I managed social media accounts and created content

Question 4: Which is more professional?

A) I graduated from Higher Colleges of Technology with a diploma in Engineering
B) I went to HCT and got my diploma
Remember:
• Use specific action verbs (managed, created, organized)
• Avoid slang words (super, stuff, lots)
• Keep sentences short and clear
• Be professional (no abbreviations like "I'm")
